          Doctrine library

          Your doctrine, SOPs, ROE cards and past lessons. The AI is given the passages that best match each request and cites them, so you can check every point against the source. Search runs in this browser and works offline.

          Documents
          Reading…
          Word (.docx), PDF with a text layer, HTML, Markdown and plain text. Headings and numbered paragraphs are kept, so citations point to the right section.
          Examples Ten invented example documents (EXCON handbook, HQ SOPs, planning, intelligence reporting, ROE card, CIMIC, media handling, sustainment and CASEVAC, an adjudication guide and a past exercise's lessons) show what works well. They're not official publications: replace them with your own.
          How to write documents that work well
          • Use headings. Start a heading line with # in text files, or use Word's Heading styles. Each heading starts a new section, and citations name it.
          • Number your paragraphs (1.1, 1.2…). Citations then point to the exact paragraph.
          • One topic per section. The AI is given the best-matching sections, not whole documents, so a focused section is found more easily.
          • Use the words your staff use: SITREP, RFI, CASEVAC, holding line. Search matches words, not meanings, so say "casualty evacuation" as well as "CASEVAC".
          • State the standard plainly, with numbers where you have them: "within 15 minutes", "below 50%", "about 3:1". The AI can then judge the players against it.
          • Check with Try a search using the words a player or controller would use.
